# Appendix F Item 7 — Inter-rater reliability source data unavailable

**Status:** Author clarification / withdrawal of unsupported reliability result. This is not a rater-score file.

## Audit finding
The manuscript reports Cohen's κ = 0.84 with a 95% confidence interval of [0.79, 0.89] from 50 assessments by two raters. The underlying paired ratings, case list, rating instructions, and reproducible calculation record could not be located. The numerical claim therefore cannot be verified and should not be reported as an established result in this version.

The supplied audit notes that another supplementary narrative mentions Krippendorff's α = 0.86 but does not include the underlying scores. That narrative statistic is not a valid substitute for a reproducible Cohen's kappa analysis. The audit also distinguishes a separate kappa value reported for clause mapping from agreement in scoring the six-domain HIMS rubric; statistics from different samples or constructs must not be interchanged.

## Proposed manuscript wording
> Inter-rater reliability of the six-domain HIMS rubric is not established by the present study because the original paired rating records and reproducible analysis output could not be located. The previously reported Cohen's kappa estimate and confidence interval are therefore withdrawn. A separate supplementary narrative mentions Krippendorff's alpha, but its underlying scores were also unavailable and that value is not treated as verified evidence. The rubric is presented as a proposed scoring instrument whose inter-rater reliability remains to be evaluated using independently collected ratings and a prespecified protocol.

## Supplementary-material action
Remove or clearly mark as withdrawn every unsupported κ/α reliability claim in the manuscript, appendices, tables, figure notes, and supplementary files. Do not publish synthetic rater scores to reproduce κ = 0.84 or α = 0.86.

The blank `templates/FUTURE_ONLY_interrater_scores_template.csv` is for new, prospectively documented scoring only. New ratings must be described as new data and must not be represented as the missing historic 50-case dataset.
