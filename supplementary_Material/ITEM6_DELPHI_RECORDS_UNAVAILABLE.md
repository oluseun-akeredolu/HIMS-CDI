# Appendix F Item 6 — Delphi voting records unavailable

**Status:** Author clarification / withdrawal of Delphi attribution. This is not a vote record.

## Audit finding
The manuscript states that the CDI threshold of 0.70 corresponds to a median acceptability rating of 3.5/5 from a six-expert Delphi panel. The original panel-by-round votes supporting that calculation could not be located. The described Delphi protocol, panel-composition information, and general narrative about rounds are not a substitute for the individual ratings needed to reproduce the claimed median.

The current archive audit also reports that the available S6 material does not document the specific 0.70 CDI threshold or the claimed 3.5/5 median. Therefore, remove the attribution of this numeric threshold to Delphi consensus unless authentic records are later recovered.

## Proposed manuscript wording
> The CDI threshold of 0.70 is retained in the Phase I baseline as a provisional design threshold used to test the gate logic. The original individual Delphi voting records supporting the previously stated median acceptability score of 3.5/5 could not be located; consequently, the threshold is not presented as Delphi-derived or expert-validated. The threshold's empirical and clinical appropriateness remains unestablished and should be assessed through a prespecified threshold-sensitivity analysis and subsequent evaluation with independently collected healthcare evidence.

The manuscript should also identify CDI ≥ 0.70, CCR ≥ 0.80, latency < 200 ms, and memory < 100 MB as **provisional Phase I decision settings**, unless each threshold has an independent, traceable source that directly supports the particular numeric limit. Do not state that DCB0129 or another clinical-safety standard establishes the exact latency or memory numbers unless a specific verified source supports that attribution.

## Supplementary-material action
Replace any implication that the threshold votes are available with this disclosure. The blank `templates/FUTURE_ONLY_Delphi_ratings_template.csv` is for a future, genuinely conducted study only; it must not be populated with reconstructed or invented historical votes.
